Spring Roll

Description

The beauty of spring rolls lies in their adaptability, but traditional versions rely on a few core ingredients for their vivid flavor, color, and texture. Here’s what you’ll need:

Ingredients

  • Rice vermicelli noodles
  • Shrimp
  • Bib or red leaf lettuce
  • Fresh herbs
  • Cucumber (optional)
  • Rice paper wrappers

Steps

  1. Cook the Noodles
  2. Prepare the Shrimp
  3. Wash and Prep Vegetables & Herbs
  4. Assemble Your Rolling Station
  5. Soften the Rice Paper
  6. Arrange the Fillings
  7. Roll the Spring Roll
  8. Repeat and enjoy!
