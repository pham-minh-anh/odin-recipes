Vietnamese Pho

Description

Savor the bold, aromatic flavors of Vietnamese beef pho made easy and fast—perfect for weeknight cooking without compromising on taste.

Ingredients

  • Beef bouillon or base
  • Vietnamese fish sauce
  • White sugar
  • Ginger
  • Kosher salt
  • Whole spices (cloves, star anise, cinnamon stick)
  • Rice stick noodles
  • Beef strip loin or fillet
  • Fresh herbs and garnishes

  2. Preparing the Noodles
  3. Assembling
  4. Enjoy!
