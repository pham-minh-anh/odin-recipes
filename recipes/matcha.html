Matcha Latte

Description

Start your day with a matcha latter for a productive mood

Ingredients

  • Matcha powder
  • Milk of choice
  • Sweetener of choice (optional)
  • Hot and cold water

Steps

  1. Whisk the matcha with cold water first and then hot water
  2. Pour milk into cup, add ice
  3. Pour the whisked matcha over the milk
  4. Enjoy!
